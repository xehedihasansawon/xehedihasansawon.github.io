Why I started it

Brand and operations, in one place.

Most small businesses get help with either their brand or their operations, rarely both. Five years of freelance design showed me the brand side; three and a half years handling production data at Square Group showed me how much time companies lose to manual work. MAAZ STUDIO brings both together. It grew directly out of my freelance work, which became the studio in February 2026.

What the studio does

Four services, one team.

Every project follows the same five steps: Discover, Plan, Design, Build and Launch. Designs are approved before any code is written, and support continues after launch.

Website DevelopmentMobile-first business sites, landing pages, e-commerce and ongoing maintenance.
MAAZ ERPSales, invoices, stock, accounts and reports in one system on a monthly plan, with Bangla support. In development, early-access waitlist open.
Branding & Business SetupLogo, brand kit, domain and email, and online profiles.
Social Media SupportPage setup, content ideas and post design for Facebook and Instagram.
Studio work so far includes the Chowdhury Overseas website, the MIUW ERP workflow system and sports identities for SSFC and Biporjoy 18.
