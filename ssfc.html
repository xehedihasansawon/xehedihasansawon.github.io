Featured Case Study · SSFC

SHAHEEN SCHOOL
FOOTBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

A multi-season football tournament design system covering social media, match graphics, jerseys, notices, event branding and large-format print — with Season 4 co-organizing responsibility.

Sports BrandingEvent DesignLarge FormatSocial Media
PROJECTSSFC · Seasons 1–4
ROLELead Graphic Designer
SEASON 4Co-Organizer
LOCATIONTangail, Bangladesh

Project Scope

One tournament, many formats.

SSFC needs graphics for different real-world uses: social posts, fixtures, match graphics, jerseys, notices, champion posts, stage backdrops, rally materials and stadium banners. The case study keeps each format in its real proportion instead of forcing everything into one fixed-size grid.

01

Stadium Outside

16 × 4 FT Banner

This artwork is a tall 1:4 layout, so it gets its own vertical presentation. Nothing is cropped or stretched.

Large Format Print1:4 RatioNo Crop
02

Stage Backdrop · All Logos

16 × 8 FT Stage Banner

A wide 2:1 stage backdrop shown at full width so team logos and event information stay readable.

03

Grand Rally

7 × 4 FT Rally Graphic

The 7:4 rally artwork stays in its natural landscape proportion for a clean, readable presentation.

Role & Responsibility

More than design.

Across Seasons 1–4 I handled the tournament's visual work. In Season 4 I was also one of the co-organizers, so the project connected design with real event planning and execution.

Visual IdentityConsistent tournament look across digital and print materials.
Match ContentFixtures, match graphics, notices, champion posts and social assets.
Event GraphicsStage, stadium, rally, banner and other large-format designs.
OrganizationSeason 4 co-organizing responsibility alongside the design work.
