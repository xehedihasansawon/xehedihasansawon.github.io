Project Overview

One workflow, connected operations.

MIUW ERP connects sourcing, inventory, order confirmation, sales, courier delivery, expenses and reporting, so a small business can run from one place instead of many disconnected spreadsheets.

01

The admin dashboard.

Core System

Built around daily business work.

Common operational tasks are grouped into clear modules: orders, courier, inventory, sourcing, expenses, accounts, team, reports and user permissions.

Sourcing & InventoryTrack incoming products, lots, stock and item-level costing.
Orders & SalesKeep order confirmation and sales activity inside one workflow.
Courier & DeliveryFollow packing, transit and delivery status without losing order context.
Finance & ReportingConnect expenses, accounts and sales for clear reports.
Private by design: this case study leaves out credentials, customer records and real business data. It explains the system through demo content only.
