About me

Designer first. Problem solver always.

the person behind the work

I’m MD Mehedi Hasan Sawon. I’ve been designing since 2020: logos, social media graphics, menus and sports identities for brands, restaurants and teams, first as a freelancer on Fiverr and since 2022 as Lead Graphic Designer of the Shaheen School Football Championship.

Alongside design, I spent three and a half years at Square Group handling production data and daily reports. Seeing how much time businesses lose to manual work is why, in February 2026, I turned my freelance work into MAAZ STUDIO: websites, ERP software and brands for growing businesses.
