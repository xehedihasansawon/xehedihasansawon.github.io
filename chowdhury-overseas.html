CHOWDHURY OVERSEAS

A bilingual website redesign for a manpower, visa and air ticket agency in Tangail. The previous developer left the site unfinished; I took it over and rebuilt it step by step into a site people can trust with their future.

The Brief

Trust comes before the visa.

People choosing a recruitment agency are making one of the biggest decisions of their lives. The site has to answer their questions quickly and look credible: which countries, which jobs, what the agency handles, and how to get in touch. And it has to work in Bangla as well as English.

01

Homepage

Your future begins here.

A clear hero with a "Request a call" action, the destination countries (Singapore, Malaysia, UAE, Qatar, Oman and Kuwait), the agency's services and its training trades, all readable on a phone.

What the site covers

Everything a candidate needs to know.

Services, countries, training and contact options are organised so a first-time visitor can find what they need without calling first.

Visa ServicesWork permit visas and visa processing, explained step by step.
Air TicketingTicket booking alongside the visa process, plus Hajj and Umrah support.
Manpower & TrainingManpower supply with training trades such as hotel, electrical, plumbing, welding and factory work.
Contact & Call-backRequest-a-call and contact forms, hotline numbers and the Tangail office address.
