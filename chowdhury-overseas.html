The Brief

Trust comes before the visa.

People choosing a recruitment agency are making one of the biggest decisions of their lives. The site has to answer their questions quickly and look credible: which countries, which jobs, what the agency handles, and how to get in touch. And it has to work in Bangla as well as English.

Section by section

What each part is for.

Real screenshots of the new site, which runs as a live preview at chowdhury-overseas.maazstudio.digital until it moves to the agency’s own domain. Each section has one job: here is what it shows and why it is there.

01

Hero

Your future begins here.

The headline, the six destination countries and two actions: Apply now and Browse jobs. Below them are three trust lines (government approved, a receipt for every taka, track your status online) and a bar of numbers: since 2020, 1,000+ workers placed, 6 countries, 7 services.

Why it’s here: Someone looking for work abroad first needs to know where they can go and whether the agency is real. The first screen answers both.

HeroTrustNumbers
02

About the agency

A real agency, real people.

The company logo with its government approval, a short history since 2020 and a message from the managing director.

Why it’s here: Recruitment fraud is a real fear. A named company, its history and a message from the person in charge build trust before any money changes hands.

AboutMD Message
03

Services

Everything needed to work abroad.

Seven colour-coded cards: work permit visa, visa processing, air ticketing, manpower supply, technical training, medical services, and Hajj and Umrah, each linking to its own page.

Why it’s here: Visitors see that the agency handles every step, from paperwork to the flight, so they don’t need several offices.

Service CardsOwn Pages
04

Destinations

Countries we send workers to.

Singapore, Malaysia, the UAE, Qatar, Oman and Kuwait, each with a skyline illustration and the main kinds of work there.

Why it’s here: Candidates think by country first. Each card answers “can I go there, and for what work?” at a glance.

CountriesIllustrations
05

How it works

Six easy steps to a new job.

Registration, documents, medical, visa, BMET clearance and flight on a numbered timeline, next to a box where candidates check their file with their passport number.

Why it’s here: Knowing every step in advance lowers worry, and the status check saves the office many phone calls.

ProcessStatus Check
06

Why choose them

Air tickets and visas, without the hassle.

Four reasons on one band: best price guarantee, a fast and simple process, 24/7 support and complete help with documents.

Why it’s here: It gives the agency’s strongest selling points one clear place, just before the jobs and training.

Benefits
07

Training & documents

Ready before you fly.

The training sectors (construction, engineering, hospitality, manufacturing, energy, and oil and gas) next to a checklist of the documents to bring to the office.

Why it’s here: A checklist means fewer wasted trips: candidates arrive with everything on their first visit.

TrainingChecklist
08

FAQ & next step

Answers before the first call.

Common questions (how long it takes, how to know a file is real, paying in instalments, what happens if a visa is rejected) and a closing band with Apply online and a phone number.

Why it’s here: Clear answers to the hard questions build trust, and the closing band gives one last easy step.

FAQContact
09

Bangla & English

One site, two languages.

The same homepage in English and in Bangla, switched from the header, with a light and a dark theme. Every page is built for phones first.

Why it’s here: Most candidates read Bangla more easily, while employers and partners abroad read English. Both get the same information.
